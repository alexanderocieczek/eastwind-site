Work

The shape of the problems we are called in for.

Six engagements, anonymised. Each started as a question and ended as something running.

Lift manufacturer

Supplier cost programme · China

Component costs had been rising for over a year and the internal cost-down effort had stalled for lack of hands. We broke the main assemblies down to component level, benchmarked the cost drivers, and renegotiated with the existing suppliers on that basis. Suppliers were then assessed on site to decide which to keep, consolidate or replace, and alternative suppliers were identified and qualified in a second country.

Outcome Fact-based prices with the current base, a consolidated supplier list, and a qualified alternative for the highest-exposure components.

Lift manufacturer

Supply chain redesign for a new product line

A shift from steel to standardised aluminium doors changed what the supply chain had to do. We mapped the existing chain step by step, from profile pressing through cutting, assembly and coating to delivery, built the volume forecast for three years, ran an RFI and RFQ with candidate suppliers, and set out which steps to outsource and which to keep in-house.

Outcome A costed supply chain recommendation with a supplier shortlist and quotations, ready to decide on.

Nordic IT reseller

Embedded supplier compliance manager · China

A growing private-label range built on Chinese suppliers, and nobody to own supplier compliance. We placed one of our people in the role: setting the audit schedule, attending every initial audit and re-audit, approving root-cause analyses and corrective actions, running supplier training, and handling code of conduct sign-off and conflict minerals due diligence, with a quarterly report back to the client.

Outcome A supplier compliance programme that runs, owned by someone the client did not have to recruit.

Private equity buyer

Supply base due diligence · China

The target's production sat with three contract manufacturers. We established legal status, ownership and actual production capability for each, through interviews and on-site inspection rather than filings.

Outcome An independent view of the investment case and of the third-party manufacturing risk, which changed where the buyer focused before signing.

Automotive supplier

Interim management · China

Compliance problems surfaced in a pre-acquisition due diligence, but nobody could say where they sat. We ran the risk assessment, found the issues across management, finance, sales and purchasing, then stepped in as interim management and restructured the organisation over eighteen months.

Outcome Purchasing costs down a quarter, sales margin up ten points, full compliance, acquisition completed.

Global trading company

Market study and target search · Vietnam

The client wanted to enter Vietnamese food retail by acquisition. We sized the market, mapped the players, set screening criteria, approached targets directly to test their appetite, and proposed a deal structure for the shortlist.

Outcome A live negotiation with an identified target, with the client supported through it.
